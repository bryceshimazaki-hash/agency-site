Our Clinicians

Amy Perry

CMHC, NCC | Clinician

I specialize in working with individuals and families impacted by domestic violence, trauma, narcissistic abuse, PTSD and other kinds of abuse. With over eight years as a certified domestic violence counselor—and additional years immersed in advocacy, mentorship and support work—I bring both professional expertise and lived experience as a partner, parent, and co-parent. Clients often find that this combination allows for a deeper level of understanding, clarity, and connection as we navigate the complexities of their experiences together.

I support clients across a wide range of situations, including leaving unsafe relationships and navigating the legal system. My work also includes guiding clients through custody concerns, creating safety plans, and rebuilding stability and identity during and after crisis.

Eric Pribyl

LCSW | Clinician

I believe that healing from trauma isn’t about “getting over it” or “pulling yourself up by your bootstraps.” It’s about reclaiming a sense of safety in your own skin. Whether you’re navigating the complexities of domestic or intimate partner violence, processing deep-seated trauma, or looking for a space where your LGBTQIA+ identity is not just respected but truly celebrated, you don’t have to do it alone.

I work with individuals ages 7+ with a focus on supporting the queer community—especially trans and gender-diverse folks—as well as those working to stop using violence in all relationships. I specialize in working with both survivors and perpetrators of domestic violence, offering a space rooted in accountability, compassion, and real change.

Elizabeth Hoffmann

LCSW | Clinician

I specialize in Narcissistic Abuse, Divorce, and trauma-focused healing for individuals and groups. I provide a structured and compassionate environment for survivors to process their experiences, build resilience, and regain a sense of self-worth after leaving high-conflict or abusive relationships.

Ellen Seely

CSW | Clinician

I am a Domestic Violence certified therapist who works with women, queer communities, people who use drugs, and individuals ages 16+, offering affirming, inclusive care grounded in values of empowerment, autonomy, and authenticity. With a strong background in public health and harm reduction, I bring specialized experience in sexual health and supporting marginalized communities.

My approach integrates solution-focused therapy, EFT, CBT, and somatic experiencing, centering on personal autonomy and client-defined success.

Bryce Shimazaki

CSW | Clinician

I view therapy as a collaborative partnership: You set the direction, and I provide the clinical tools to help you get there. My approach is rooted in the belief that many “struggles” began as survival skills. Together, we’ll honor how you’ve protected yourself while building new patterns that align with your values—moving from survival toward a more intentional, authentic life.

Using evidence-based practices such as DBT, ACT, CBT, mindfulness, and psychoeducation, I help clients develop emotional regulation, psychological flexibility, and a stronger sense of agency.

Megan Thomson

CMHC, NCC | Clinician

Therapy is often described as a difficult and raw process—and at times, it truly is. I am passionate about working with adults and teenagers (12+) who are ready to learn, heal, and grow. I commonly support clients navigating PTSD, anxiety, depression, domestic violence, ADHD, and trauma.

I strive to provide a safe, supportive, and non-judgmental space where you can explore what matters most to you. My approach integrates evidence-based modalities such as DBT, CBT, and ACT.

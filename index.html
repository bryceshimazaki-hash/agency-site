Amy Perry

I specialize in working with individuals and families impacted by domestic violence, trauma, narcissistic abuse, PTSD and other kinds of abuse. I bring both professional expertise and lived experience as a partner, parent, and co-parent.

I support clients leaving unsafe relationships and navigating the legal system, integrating somatic experiencing, DBT, and EMDR to help clients process trauma and move toward a more stable, self-directed life.

×

Bryce Shimazaki

I view therapy as a collaborative partnership: You set the direction, and I provide the clinical tools to help you get there. My approach is rooted in the belief that many struggles began as survival skills.

Using evidence-based practices such as DBT, ACT, and mindfulness, I help clients develop emotional regulation and a stronger sense of agency in their path toward intentional living.

×

Eric Pribyl

Healing from trauma is about reclaiming a sense of safety in your own skin. I work with individuals ages 7+ with a focus on supporting the queer community—especially trans and gender-diverse folks.

I specialize in working with both survivors and perpetrators of domestic violence, offering a space rooted in accountability and compassion.

Ellen Seely

I am a Domestic Violence certified therapist offering affirming, inclusive care grounded in empowerment and authenticity. My approach addresses the underlying root causes of distress while centering on personal autonomy.

Megan Thomson

I am passionate about working with adults and teenagers who are ready to learn, heal, and grow. I support clients navigating PTSD, anxiety, depression, and trauma using DBT, CBT, and somatic approaches.

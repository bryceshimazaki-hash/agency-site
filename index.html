Our Clinicians

Amy Perry

CMHC, NCC | Clinician

I specialize in working with individuals and families impacted by domestic violence, trauma, narcissistic abuse, PTSD and other kinds of abuse. With over eight years as a certified domestic violence counselor—and additional years immersed in advocacy, mentorship and support work—I bring both professional expertise and lived experience as a partner, parent, and co-parent. Clients often find that this combination allows for a deeper level of understanding, clarity, and connection as we navigate the complexities of their experiences together.

I support clients across a wide range of situations, including growing up in abusive environments, leaving unsafe relationships, and navigating the legal system. My approach integrates modalities such as somatic experiencing, DBT, EMDR (1.0 and 2.0), and psychoeducation to help clients process trauma and move toward a more stable, self-directed life.

Eric Pribyl

LCSW | Clinician

Healing from trauma isn’t about “getting over it” or “pulling yourself up by your bootstraps.” It’s about reclaiming a sense of safety in your own skin. Whether you’re navigating domestic violence or looking for a space where your LGBTQIA+ identity is truly celebrated, you don’t have to do it alone.

I work with individuals ages 7+ with a focus on supporting the queer community—especially trans and gender-diverse folks. I specialize in working with both survivors and perpetrators of domestic violence, offering a space rooted in accountability, compassion, and real change.

Elizabeth Hoffmann

LCSW | Clinician

I specialize in Narcissistic Abuse, Divorce, and trauma-focused healing for individuals and groups. I provide a structured and compassionate environment for survivors to process their experiences, build resilience, and regain a sense of self-worth after leaving high-conflict or abusive relationships.

Ellen Seely

CSW | Clinician

I am a Domestic Violence certified therapist offering affirming, inclusive care grounded in values of empowerment and authenticity. With a background in public health and harm reduction, I bring specialized experience in supporting marginalized communities.

My approach integrates multiple modalities, addresssing the underlying root causes of distress, and centers on personal autonomy, dignity, and client-defined success.

Bryce Shimazaki

CSW | Clinician

I view therapy as a collaborative partnership: You set the direction, and I provide the clinical tools to help you get there. My approach is rooted in the belief that many “struggles” began as survival skills.

Together, we’ll honor how you’ve protected yourself while building new patterns that align with your values—moving from survival toward a more intentional, authentic life.

Amy Perry

I specialize in working with individuals and families impacted by domestic violence, trauma, narcissistic abuse, PTSD and other kinds of abuse. I bring professional expertise and lived experience as a partner, parent, and co-parent. I support clients leaving unsafe relationships and navigating the legal system, integrating somatic experiencing, DBT, and EMDR.

Bryce Shimazaki

I view therapy as a collaborative partnership. I provide clinical tools to help you set the direction. My approach is rooted in the belief that many struggles began as survival skills. Using evidence-based practices such as DBT, ACT, and mindfulness, I help clients develop emotional regulation and agency.

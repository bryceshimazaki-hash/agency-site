Amy Perry

I specialize in working with individuals and families impacted by domestic violence, trauma, narcissistic abuse, PTSD and other kinds of abuse. With over eight years as a certified domestic violence counselor—and additional years immersed in advocacy, mentorship and support work—I bring both professional expertise and lived experience as a partner, parent, and co-parent.

I support clients across a wide range of situations, including growing up in abusive environments, leaving unsafe relationships, and navigating the legal system. My work also includes guiding clients through custody concerns and rebuilding stability and identity during and after crisis.

My approach is grounded in empowerment, honesty, and practical support, integrating somatic experiencing, DBT, and EMDR.

Bryce Shimazaki

I view therapy as a collaborative partnership: Clients set the direction, and I provide the clinical tools to help clients get there. My approach is rooted in the belief that many “struggles” began as survival skills.

Together, you’ll honor how you’ve protected yourself while building new patterns that align with your values—moving from survival toward a more intentional, authentic life.

Using evidence-based practices such as DBT, ACT, CBT, and mindfulness, I help clients develop emotional regulation and a stronger sense of agency.

Eric Pribyl

I believe that healing from trauma isn’t about “getting over it” or “pulling yourself up by your bootstraps.” It’s about reclaiming a sense of safety in your own skin. I work with individuals ages 7+ with a focus on supporting the queer community—especially trans and gender-diverse folks.

I specialize in working with both survivors and perpetrators of domestic violence, offering a space rooted in accountability, compassion, and real change. My approach is person-first and somatic-focused, honoring how your experiences live in both your mind and body.

Ellen Seely

I am a Domestic Violence certified therapist who works with women, queer communities, people who use drugs, and individuals ages 16+, offering affirming care grounded in values of empowerment and authenticity.

Over the past several years, I have provided compassionate support to individuals navigating stigma, trauma, and complex life challenges. My approach integrates solution-focused therapy, EFT, CBT, and somatic experiencing.

Megan Thomson

Therapy is often described as a difficult process, but it can also be energizing and motivating. I am passionate about working with adults and teenagers (12+) ready to learn, heal, and grow.

I support clients navigating PTSD, anxiety, depression, and domestic violence. My approach integrates DBT, CBT, and somatic approaches to build a natural, authentic connection with each client.
